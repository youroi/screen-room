## Why

The weekly trend feed includes upcoming titles and previews. The catalogue should show titles with a regular public release, including theatrical releases.

## What Changes

- Verify regular theatrical or digital movie releases and aired series episodes before publishing Popular and Trending.
- Include theatrical debuts in Latest Releases alongside digital debuts.
- Publish all four refreshed shelves together and retain the previous snapshot on a failed refresh.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `catalogue`: Discovery shelves use verified public release eligibility.

## Impact

The existing Pages workflow refreshes the release-checked snapshot on its first deployment. Playback and title routes keep using their existing sources.
