## Why

Home repeats four static featured titles and cuts discovery rows off before relevant regional titles can appear. Latest Releases also mixes theatrical dates with digital debuts.

## What Changes

- Select six featured titles from released movies, series, and animation. Give recent browser playback verification priority.
- Keep six discovery rows with a maximum of 30 titles each. Base Popular on global and regional popularity, preserve weekly Trending order, and require a verified regional digital debut for Latest.
- Preserve Quiet Cinema, keep official Hindi dub records season-specific, and keep playback lookup behind Play.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `catalogue`: Update the Home hero, discovery rows, and refresh rules.

## Impact

The same-origin catalogue snapshot and its GitHub Actions workflow change. Playback and public navigation keep their existing entry points.
