## Decisions

- Eligible movies have a past TMDB regular theatrical or digital release. Festival premieres, limited previews, and future dates are excluded.
- Eligible TV shows have an aired episode in a numbered season. Preserve source order while skipping ineligible titles.
- Latest checks Bangladesh, India, and the United States in that order. Each market uses the more recent first theatrical opening or first digital debut with a regional provider listing.
- Home loads one versioned snapshot for Popular, Trending, and Latest. Curated shelves and the editorial hero render independently.
