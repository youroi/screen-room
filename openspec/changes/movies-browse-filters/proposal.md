## Why

Movies needs reliable combined Country and Genre browsing with the existing Quiet Cinema styling. A homepage shelf cannot supply sufficient results or the production countries of co-productions.

## What Changes

- Add a Movies-only filter rail and responsive poster grid with URL-backed selections.
- Publish a broad, popularity-ordered Movies index in paged shards, joined on actual production country and genre.
- Preserve the selected page and scroll position when returning from details.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `movies-browse`: Browse paginated released movies by production country and genre.

## Impact

Movies receives its own catalogue data. The existing Movies tab, details route and Play flow remain its entry points.
